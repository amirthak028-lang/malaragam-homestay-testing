02 · REFUND POLICY

Cancellation & refunds

The advance payment refund depends on when the cancellation request is received.

48 hours or more before check-in100% of the advance payment is eligible for refund.
Less than 48 hours before check-inNo advance refund.
No-showNo refund.
Cancellation by Malaragam100% of the advance payment is eligible for refund.
Booking not approved by MalaragamFull advance payment refund.

The request timestamp is used to assess the 48-hour deadline. Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method. Bank and payment-provider processing times may vary.
