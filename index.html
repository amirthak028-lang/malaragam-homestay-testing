Questions guests ask

Do I book a single room or the whole home?

The whole home. Both bedrooms, both bathrooms, the hall and (with the kitchen package) the kitchen are yours alone.

How many people can stay?

Up to 6 guests, children included. Extra beds are ₹500 each per night.

How does payment work?

You pay 50% online to confirm your dates. The remaining 50% is settled directly with Malaragam.

Can I book more than one night?

Yes. Each night is one 24-hour stay, and you can book several in a row.

Is food included?

No, meals are not included in the pricing. The kitchen-access package lets you cook, with basic utensils provided.

What if I do not finish paying?

Your dates are held for 15 minutes while you pay. After that they are released for other guests.
